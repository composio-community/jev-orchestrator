# FAQ

<!-- Jev only uses "faq" when the answer is actually in here. One question per heading. -->

## What does it cost?
$29/month per location, unlimited staff and bookings. 14-day free trial, no card needed.

## Do you offer refunds?
Yes. Cancel within the first 30 days of a paid plan and we refund the full month, no questions asked.

## Do you send SMS reminders?
Yes, SMS and email reminders are included. SMS works in the US, UK, Canada and Australia.

## Can clients pay when they book?
Yes, connect Stripe in Settings → Payments. We take no cut beyond Stripe's fee.
